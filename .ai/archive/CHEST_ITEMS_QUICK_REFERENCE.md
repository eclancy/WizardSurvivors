## Quick Tips

### Early Game (0-5 items)
- Prioritize survival: Aegis Sigil, Basalt Carapace
- Or pick one path: Damage (Ember + Wrath) or Speed (Quicksilver + Haste)

### Mid Game (5-10 items)
- Complete first synergy: Vaultguard or Emberline are easiest
- Start second path that shares items
- Build toward Eternal Guardian if tanking

### Late Game (10+ items)
- Complete 2-3 synergies for massive bonuses
- Pick archetypal synergies: Deathbringer, Speed Demon, Elemental Mastery
- Mix and match: Fortune's Favor scales all other items

### Most Powerful Combinations
1. Deathbringer (2 items, execute dmg)
2. Elemental Mastery (3 items, 40% potency)
3. Eternal Guardian (3 items, tank stats)
4. Stormbound (3 items, crit + speed)

### Best Value (Items per Bonus)
1. Fortune's Favor (2 items = 25% drops + 25% XP)
2. Speed Demon (2 items = 20% speed + 15% attack speed)
3. Deathbringer (2 items = 25% damage + execute)

### Most Expensive (But Powerful)
1. Eternal Guardian (3 items = +80 HP + 18% DR)
2. Elemental Mastery (3 items = 40% potency + effects)
